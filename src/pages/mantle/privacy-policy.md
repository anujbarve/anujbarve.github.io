## Introduction

Mantle ("the App") is a developer snippet manager developed by Anuj Barve. This privacy policy explains how the App handles your data.

## Data Collection

Mantle **does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data**.

- No account creation or login required
- No analytics or crash reporting SDKs
- No advertising or tracking frameworks
- No network requests to external servers
- No email, name, location, or device identifiers collected

## Data Storage

All data — snippets, favorites, theme preferences, and settings — is stored **locally on your device** using Hive (an embedded NoSQL database). No data is sent to any remote server or third party.

## Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or data processing services.

## Data Sharing

The App does not share any user data with third parties because no user data is collected.

## Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally, deleting the App from your device will remove all associated data. You can also clear custom snippets from within the App's Settings screen.

## Children's Privacy

The App is intended for general audiences (rated Everyone) and does not knowingly collect any data from children.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top will be revised. Since the App operates fully offline, no push notification of changes is possible — please check this page periodically.
