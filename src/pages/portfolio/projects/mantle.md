**Mantle** is a fast, minimal, offline-first developer snippet manager. No login, no ads, no internet required — just a clean place to store every command, shortcut, and code pattern you don't want to forget.

Built with **Flutter** and **Dart**, it uses **Hive** for local storage, keeping all data on your device with zero network dependencies.

## 🧩 Features

### Browse by category
Organized into Git, Docker, Flutter, Laravel, Linux, Python, and more. Each category has its own brand color for quick visual scanning.

### Search instantly
Full-screen search with filter chips. Results highlight your query in the app's accent color. Find what you need in seconds.

### Save favorites
Star the snippets you use most. Access them in one tap from the Library tab.

### Add your own
Create custom snippets for your personal workflows. Edit or delete them anytime.

### Copy with one tap
Every snippet has a dedicated copy button. Mantle tracks your recently copied snippets so you can quickly reuse what's fresh.

### Dark first, light when you want
Pitch-black dark mode with Dracula syntax highlighting. Switch to light mode in Settings. Choose from 6 accent colors — Purple, Green, Cyan, Pink, Orange, or Yellow.

### Fully offline
All snippets are stored locally on your device. No account, no data collection, no internet connection needed.

## 💡 Technologies used

- Flutter (Dart)
- Hive (Local Storage)

## 🎯 Objective

Mantle was built to solve a simple problem: every developer has a growing collection of commands, shortcuts, and code patterns they keep re-Googling. Mantle gives you a single, offline place to store them — no accounts, no internet, no friction.
